Card Night Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. This policy covers the Card Night app for iPhone.

Short version

Card Night has no account and no server. The names you type, your answers, favorites and progress stay on your phone. We do not collect them, sell them or share them.

What stays on your phone

  • Player names you add.
  • Cards you played, favorites, notes and saved date ideas.
  • Your question-of-the-day streak and simple counters (for example, how many cards you played).

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear it in Settings > Reset progress.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own. RevenueCat receives your purchase records and a random ID created for this app. It does not receive your names or answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn them on, the app schedules reminders on your phone: a daily question reminder, and a reminder one day before a free trial ends. These are created on your device.

Children

The app is rated 13+. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
