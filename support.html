I paid but the packs are still locked

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you paid with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Card Night, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is the free trial really free?

The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial if your Apple Account has not used it before. If you allow notifications, the app reminds you the day before the trial ends.

Where are my names and answers stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account. See the privacy policy.
